Work Hour Tracker (2025)

A simple, interactive HTML-based work hour tracker for 2025. This tool allows you to select any month of the year, mark the days you've worked, and automatically calculates your total work hours. It's perfect for freelancers, remote workers, or anyone who wants to keep track of their monthly working hours.

✨ Features

📅 Displays the calendar for the current month and year on load.
✅ Click on any day to mark it as worked (default: 8 hours).
✏️ Click the pencil icon on a marked day to edit the hours for that specific day.
👤 Personalize with your name and adjust the default daily hours.
🔄 Navigate between months using arrow buttons.
📊 Automatically updates and displays your monthly total hours in the summary.
🧾 Export your report to PDF, showing all details including:
Name
Month & year
Worked dates and their hours
Total number of worked days
Total hours
📦 How to Use

Open the index.html file in your browser.
Enter your name and default daily hours (optional).
Click on the days you worked to toggle them.
For partial work days, click the pencil icon on a marked day and enter custom hours.
View your total hours in the Work Summary.
Click Export PDF to download a professional summary report.
📝 Note

This tracker is built using pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with no external dependencies. The export-to-PDF feature generates a custom report using the collected data and presents it in a clean, printable format.
